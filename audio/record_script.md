# Recording script (items with no public-domain audio)

**280 items, about 96 minutes of speech** (plus pauses: budget 2-3 hours of studio time, or 1 hour for a fast reader).

## How to record

- Quiet room, phone or USB mic 15 cm away, WAV or M4A. One calm, steady voice for the whole channel (brand voice).
- Read the text exactly as written, including the old words (thou, thy). Do not add or skip words: the audio is matched to the text automatically.
- Say the item ID, pause 1 second, then read the text, then pause 2 seconds. Example: *EP-EN-05a ... (pause) ... text ...*. One long file per section is fine.
- Skip anything in [brackets]; read display text only. If you stumble, pause and re-read the whole item (the matcher keeps the best take).

The IDs are cut out of the final clip automatically.
